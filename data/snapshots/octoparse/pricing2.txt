Learn more in our help center or contact support . How much does Octoparse cost? Octoparse starts at $0 with a free forever plan that includes 10 tasks and 50,000 rows of monthly data export. Paid plans begin at $69/month for the Standard plan and $249/month for the Professional plan, both billed annually with 16% savings versus monthly billing. Enterprise pricing is custom. All paid Octoparse plans include a 5-day money-back guarantee. Is there a free version of Octoparse? Yes. The Octoparse Free plan is free forever with no credit card required. You get 10 scraping tasks, 1 device, local extraction, and up to 50,000 rows of data export per month to Excel, CSV, JSON, HTML, or XML. It's designed for personal projects, learning web scraping, and evaluating Octoparse before upgrading to a paid plan. What's the difference between the Standard and Professional plans? The Standard Plan (from $69/month) is designed for small teams and includes 100 tasks, cloud extraction with up to 3 concurrent cloud processes, 500+ preset scraping templates, IP rotation, residential proxies, automatic CAPTCHA solving, unlimited data export, task scheduling, and Data Export API access.
The Professional Plan ($249/month) is built for medium-sized businesses scaling their web scraping operations. It includes everything in Standard, plus Pay-Per-Result Template Discount, 250 tasks, up to 20 concurrent cloud processes, cloud task monitoring, the ability to save data directly to Google Sheets / Google Drive / Dropbox / S3, auto backup data to cloud, Advanced API, priority support, and task review with 1-on-1 training.
In short, Standard gives you the core anti-blocking and scheduling toolkit for recurring jobs, while Professional adds the scale, monitoring, cloud integrations, and dedicated support needed to run larger, business-critical scraping workflows. Do you offer discounts for annual billing? Yes. You save 16% by choosing annual billing over monthly billing on any paid Octoparse plan. Annual billing also unlocks the full feature set of each tier with no monthly commitment overhead. Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied with Octoparse? Yes. All paid Octoparse plans come with a 5-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied within the first 5 days of your subscription, contact support@octoparse.com to request a full refund. Octoparse offers a 30% startup discount for one year, plus special pricing for students, educators, and non-profit organizations.
