What counts as a Successful Render? Unlike other APIs we don't charge for requests that fail for any reason. This happens when a web page is unavailble or the combination of options sent to us is not valid. A Successful Render is one that results in an image being returned to you. Do you cache screenshots? Yes, we cache screenshots for 30 days when generated with Render Links. You can override this setting via the TTL parameter or use the force option to override a previously cached screenshot. Do requests to cached screenshots count against the monthly quota on the plan? No. Only screenshots that are being rendered for the first time or via our REST API count against the quota. What happens if I go over my quota? No need to worry - we won't cut off your service. We'll automatically upgrade you to the next tier and you benefit from volume pricing. See the pricing page for more details. Do you send alerts when I am breaching or close to going over my monthly quota of screenshots? We sure do. We will send you an email when you are at 80%, 90%, and 100% usage. Are my credit card details safe? Yes. We never store or transmit your credit card details to our servers. We outsource all of the payment and subscription handling to our payment processor, Stripe. Do you offer any support? Of course! Feel free to try our first-class service out. We generally respond to any queries via email within the same day, and we aim to resolve issues within 3 working days.
