## Frequently Asked  Questions

What is an API credit?

An API credit is an HTTP request to any of our endpoints considered as a single request. The API credit is counted only when it is executed and displays a result, it won’t count if the parameters or filters are wrongly mentioned, or you didn’t get any result.

What are monthly and yearly pricing?

NewsData.io has two payment options where you get to choose if you want to make monthly payments or yearly payments. If you choose monthly payments, you will be billed for your chosen plan every month. The monthly plan renews automatically every month.

If you choose the yearly pricing plan, you will be required to make a pre-payment for 12 months. The yearly plan comes inclusive with a 20% discount.

Other than Credit/debit cards, what other payment options do i have?

If you are unable to make the payment through our default payment gateway and require any other payment method, kindly drop us a mail at [contact@newsdata.io](https://newsdata.io/contact) and we will explore alternative payment options for you.

We accept payments through Paypal, Stripe, Razorpay, and Payoneer.

What if my requests aren’t consumed till the month end?

The number of requests is refreshed each month. If you have some requests remaining at the month-end, they will lapse. A remaining number of requests won’t be carried over to the next month and you will get a fresh number of requests as per your plan in the next month.

Do you require Credit/debit card details for the free plan?

No Credit/debit card details required for the free plan.

How can I upgrade my plan?

Upgrading your plan is super easy. Simply log in to your account and choose a different pricing plan. Your new plan will be activated immediately once you make the payment.

What if my question is not in the FAQs?

What about cancellations? Can I cancel my plan at any time?

It would be sad to see you go but yes, you can cancel your plan anytime you want. Once you cancel the subscription, you will not be billed for the subsequent months. However, if any trackers are active on your account, they will continue to collect data till the end of your subscription validity

What if my request quota gets finished during my pricing plan validity?

Every pricing plan comes with a number of requests that can be consumed. If your request quota is about to get depleted, you can add requests to the existing plan by contacting us at [contact@newsdata.io.](https://newsdata.io/contact)

How can I downgrade my plan?

To **downgrade your current plan,** please reach out to our support team at [hello@newsdata.io](mailto:hello@newsdata.io) — we’ll be happy to assist you.

If you prefer to **cancel your subscription,** you can do so anytime directly from your **Dashboard** by clicking on **“Cancel Subscription”.**This will stop automatic renewal and ensure that no further charges are applied.

How many credits does the Archive endpoint use?

Each Archive endpoint call uses 5 API credits, regardless of the filters applied or the number of articles returned.
