 ## What you get # 
 /scrape URL → clean Markdown, boilerplate stripped, ready for a prompt. 
 /extract URL + a plain-English ask → typed JSON records. No selectors. 
 /search query → ranked results as JSON - add rich=1 for follow-ups, related & more. 
 /answer a question → a direct answer with its sources. Experimental. 
 /serp query → Google's results page as data: true ranks, every block, localised to a place. For rank tracking. Beta since 8 Oct 2026. 
 at scale queues, jobs, retries, and solid proxies - handled for you.
