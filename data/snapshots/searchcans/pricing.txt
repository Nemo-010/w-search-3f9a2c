 FAQ: API credits, billing, and throughput How are credits consumed, and do they expire? Credits are valid for 6 months . Standard Google and Bing search requests use 1 credit each . Reader API standard mode uses 2 credits; Shared Pool with proxy:1 uses 4 credits total . Some advanced options use additional credits. One credit balance works across Search and Reader APIs, and credits are deducted only for successful HTTP 200 responses . See the credit-usage table below for the full breakdown. How does billing work, and what payment methods do you accept? Current SearchCans credit packs are prepaid one-time purchases and do not renew automatically . Buy credits for the workload you have now, then add credits when needed. Purchases support credit card payments through Stripe. Enterprise customers can contact us about invoice payments. How reliable is the SearchCans API? SearchCans targets 99.9% monthly uptime . This service-level objective is not a guarantee or warranty ; check the status page for current service availability. Can I get a refund? Refund requests are eligible within 7 calendar days of payment when you have used less than 10% of the applicable plan credits . Approved refunds are subject to the fees described in Terms of Service Section 4.3 . Is the data provided through your API sourced legally? Yes. We only extract publicly accessible search results using lawful, industry-standard technical methods. We never use illegal cracking techniques.
