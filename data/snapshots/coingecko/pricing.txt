 Is CoinGecko API free?
 CoinGecko API offers both free and paid plans. The Demo API plan is accessible to all CoinGecko users at zero cost, with a stable rate limit of 100 calls/min and a monthly cap of 10,000 calls. Paid plans start at $35/mo, offering a higher rate limit of 300 calls/min and a monthly cap of 100,000 calls.
 What is the CoinGecko API?
 CoinGecko API is a comprehensive, industry-trusted crypto data API spanning centralized, onchain, and tokenized markets. It gives developers, projects, and enterprises access to reliable market data across crypto assets, DEXs, tokenized stocks, commodities, and other real-world assets through one unified, scalable API. 
What sets CoinGecko’s API apart is its independence and reputation for data accuracy. Unlike exchange-owned data providers, CoinGecko’s incentives are aligned with data accuracy rather than trade volume. Our crypto assets are independently verified, with prices aggregated across centralized and decentralized exchanges using our published methodology . 
The CoinGecko API is widely recognized as the most trusted source of token legitimacy and market data in the industry, with some of the best market and onchain DEX coverage available.
 What are the rate limits for CoinGecko’s API?
 The CoinGecko API Demo plan has a rate limit of 100 calls/min, while paid plans offer a higher rate limit of 300 to 2,500 calls/min. Get in touch with our Enterprise Sales team if you’d like to explore a custom plan with flexible rate limits, dedicated account management, integration consultation, and strategic data advisory.
 Can I get both live and historical crypto prices with CoinGecko API?
 Yes. CoinGecko API provides real-time and historical market data across crypto and tokenized assets. For crypto, you can use endpoints such as /simple/price and /coins/markets , or stream low-latency price updates through CoinGecko’s WebSocket API . 
Historical crypto data is available through endpoints such as /coins/{id}/market_chart and /coins/{id}/history , with up to 13+ years of price, market cap, and volume data (subject to data availability and your API subscription plan). 
For tokenized real-world assets, dedicated RWA endpoints provide live market data, while paid plans unlock historical price, market cap, and volume data for deeper analysis and backtesting.
 Does CoinGecko API provide real-world asset (RWA) and tokenized asset data?
 Yes. CoinGecko API provides market data for tokenized real-world assets, including tokenized stocks, commodities, and other RWAs. You can discover supported assets, access price, market cap, and volume data, compare underlying tokens and issuers, find trading markets across CEXs and DEXs, and retrieve historical market data through dedicated RWA endpoints .
 Can I access onchain DEX data from CoinGecko API?
 Yes! You can access onchain DEX data through more than 20 endpoints on the CoinGecko API. Key datasets include crypto liquidity pools, token data by contract address and OHLCV chart data. 
This data is powered by our sister product, GeckoTerminal, which monitors real-time crypto price, trading volume, transactions, liquidity and more, on 2,000+ exchanges across 360+ blockchain networks – including popular platforms like Uniswap, PancakeSwap, Orca, Curve, and Balancer.
 What are some examples of onchain data provided by CoinGecko API?
 CoinGecko API provides rich onchain market data for 49M+ tokens traded across 360+ blockchain networks, including Ethereum, Solana, TON, BNB Chain, and Base. Some popular onchain data types include: 
 Token Prices & Liquidity Data: Get real-time and historical token prices, liquidity, transactions, and more from DEXs 
 OHLCV Data : Build candlestick charts using open, high, low, close, and volume data with second-level resolution 
 Launchpad Tokens : Get bonding curve data for tokens on Pump.fun, Four.meme, Raydium Launchlab and more 
 Security Info: Fetch metrics like GeckoTerminal Score, holder distribution, and mint/freeze authority to verify token legitimacy

 1 call = 1 credit. Calling any endpoint will only count as 1 call.
 When does my request volume (monthly API credit) reset?
 Regardless of your billing cycle, your requests are counted from the first day of the month to the last.

 What are the differences between Demo, Basic, Analyst, Lite, Pro, Pro+ and Enterprise Plans?
 The Demo Plan is a free plan to try out the CoinGecko API. It’s good for testing and exploration, but comes with limited call credits and a rate limit of 100 calls/min. 
 The Basic Plan is suited for personal use that requires more call credits, a higher rate limit, and more frequent data updates.

 Can I stream real-time data via WebSocket?
 Yes, CoinGecko WebSockets serve two primary types of real-time data streams:
 Aggregated Global Market Pricing - Unlike single-exchange feeds that stream unverified raw ticks from a single venue, our aggregated pricing engine continuously ingests live trading data from 2,000+ global exchanges.  Onchain DEX Data - Delivers direct streams of DEX pool transactions and token price movements as they land onchain via GeckoTerminal, providing immediate visibility into DEX liquidity and trading events.
