Common Crawl is a 501(c)(3) non–profit founded in 2007. ‍ We make wholesale extraction, transformation, and analysis of open web data accessible to researchers. Overview Over 300 billion pages spanning 15 years. Free and open corpus since 2007. Cited in over 10,000 research papers. 3–5 billion new pages added each month.
