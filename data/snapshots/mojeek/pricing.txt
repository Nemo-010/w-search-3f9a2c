 FAQ 
 Is there a free Search API available for testing? 
 Yes, sign up to our Startup Plan and claim a free credit. A bank card is required to open an account however no automatic payments are processed and you can cancel anytime. Check out our Quickstart guide with an example of recommended parameters to start testing. 
 Can I see your Terms of Service Agreement? 
 Yes, please read our Search API Terms of Service Agreement which covers our services, permissions, exclusions, warranties etc. These terms are also available when you open your Mojeek Search API account. 
 What data outputs does the Search API provide? 
 Our Search API is used across a broad range of use cases with results consisting of URL, page title and query dependent snippets. These are customisable by country, language, ranking, clustering and more so see our Documentation to configure results for your project. 
 Can I retain or store data outside of my account? 
 Yes, our Business Plan and Enterprise Plan have storage rights which permit indefinite data retention. The Startup Plan enables you to cache results for one hour so, if you need storage rights, simply upgrade to our Business Plan. 
 Can I combine search results with other data sources? 
 Yes, you can combine our Search API results with other data sources such as other web search suppliers. You can also modify the data however please read our Terms of Service Agreement for all permissions and exclusions. 
 Can I display advertising alongside Search API results? 
 Yes, you’re welcome to display search results alongside third party advertising. We don’t require attribution however please see Terms of Service Agreement for all the details. 
 Can I use search results for commercial purposes? 
 Yes, you can use our Search API for commercial reasons, such as your own subscription service, as long as you adhere to our Terms of Service Agreement and policies. 
 How are payments processed for your Search API? 
 Our PAYG plans do not process automatic payments and you can set alerts on your account to control your budget. Payments are processed by Stripe Payments Europe Ltd and you can cancel anytime. Invoicing is available on Enterprise Plans. 
 Are any sub-processors used to deliver results? 
 No, our infrastructure is owned and operated in Europe with no sub-processors or third parties used in the provision of search results. Mojeek Search API is the “Processor” and you’re the “Controller” when processing queries. 
 Do I need to consider any copyright laws? 
 Our Search API provides publicly available URLs, page titles and query dependent snippets from webpages that don’t block Mojeek from crawling or indexing them. We do not grant any rights to use third party content on these webpages so customers must comply with publisher copyright terms.
