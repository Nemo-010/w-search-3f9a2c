 FAQ Have more questions? Contact us What is Anchor Browser? Anchor is a cloud-hosted browser that allows AI agents to interact with the web just like a human would. It provides secure, authenticated environments where AI can navigate web pages, submit forms, and extract data in real-time. It's designed to help organizations automate web-based tasks that aren't exposed via traditional APIs. When should I use this? Use Anchor Browser when you need AI agents to perform complex actions on web interfaces that do not have an API, or when dealing with websites that require human-like interaction, such as handling cookies, form submissions, and navigating through pages with authentication or session management. How is this different from other AI-web-interface products? Anchor Browser stands out by offering a more human-like browsing experience for AI agents, emphasizing security and flexibility. It provides customizable environments that can handle authenticated applications and complex workflows, while ensuring scalability and ease of integration. It’s specifically designed for enterprise use, balancing powerful AI interaction capabilities with stringent anti-abuse and user management mechanisms. Ready to build reliable automations?
