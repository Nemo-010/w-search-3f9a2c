 Frequently asked questions (FAQs)​
 What counts as a page? 
 A page refers to a webpage, and we provide a breakdown of it here . For billing purposes, a page is considered an HTTP request, which usually involves an HTTP GET or POST request, but it could involve more. If a redirect request is initiated, it could trigger additional page requests, and if there are AJAX or XHR requests, they could also trigger more page requests. 
 Can we run a paid trial or POC to prove an idea? 
 Yes. If you are unsure about the viability of a concept or idea, we do run Pilot projects/ Proof of Concept/ trials, which are bound by cost, time, and deliverables. In most cases, you can validate your idea in just a few weeks using a few hundred dollars. 
 Do I need to download software or build my own scraper? 
 No, our “full service” includes everything. You do not need to download software, develop scrapers or worry about getting blocked, proxies, servers, etc. We handle all the work for you, and you simply receive the data from us. 
 What is the difference between the On-Demand and Subscription Plans? 
 The On-Demand plan is for one-off data needs. If you have a one-time data requirement or if you need the data less frequently, say whenever you want a data refresh, the On-Demand plan is a good option for you. However, if you require data on a regular basis, our subscription plans offer the most cost-effective per-unit pricing. Keep in mind that subscription plans require a monthly commitment. 
 Why is a setup fee required? 
 We need to program and build software according to your exact specifications, data fields, and data format. Then, we need to test it and perform Quality Assurance (QA) to ensure it meets your needs. All these human-intensive actions are included in our nominal setup fees. 
 What’s included in the monthly subscription? 
 Our monthly subscription includes all the costs associated with data gathering – i.e., servers, bandwidth, proxy costs, etc. It also includes continuous monitoring for website structure changes and handling the most common changes. We ensure high-quality data by performing regular data quality checks, and we provide ongoing support to help you get the most out of our services. 
 Do you work with corporate procurement, accounting, legal and compliance departments? 
 Yes. We work with large global organizations to build custom contracts and handle various corporate payment methods. We also include legal and compliance groups in our workflow to ensure our customers are comfortable with how we gather data. 
 Can we outsource our in-house operations to scrapehero? 
 Yes. We specialize in taking over in-house scraping operations, whether they are small or large-scale. We work with you to build a customized transition plan that ensures a seamless handover without any disruption to your ongoing operations. Our clients often approach us because their in-house operations have become too expensive, difficult to maintain, and have multiple challenges that arise on a daily basis.
