 Driving the future of Open Research 
 OpenAIRE is more than services. It is a force for systemic change in how research is shared, assessed, and reused. Through a strong European network and growing global partnerships , we build bridges across borders, powering core EOSC infrastructure and shaping the EOSC Federation. We advance Responsible Research Assessment aligned with CoARA , promote FAIR data worldwide, and reinforce Europe’s leadership in open, trusted, community-driven research infrastructure.
