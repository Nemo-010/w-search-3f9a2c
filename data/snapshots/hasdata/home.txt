That's how we hold 99.9% uptime. Read Article -> FAQ Frequently Asked Questions Everything you need to know about web scraping with HasData. What is HasData? HasData is a web scraping service for collecting structured JSON or Markdown from public websites. It offers 63 API endpoints and 21 available no-code scrapers, with prices starting at $0.08 per 1,000 requests. The cost depends on the endpoint and request settings. Can I use both no-code scrapers and APIs with one subscription? Yes, your subscription can be used flexibly across all our products. You can access no-code scrapers, scraping APIs, or a combination of both. No separate plans or subscriptions needed. Can I try HasData for free before subscribing? Yes, the free plan includes 1,000 credits every month. No credit card is required. Can I cancel or change my subscription at any time? Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time directly from your account dashboard. Changes take effect immediately. What is an API credit, and how are they used? An API credit is HasData's internal unit for measuring the computational cost of a scraping request. The number of credits used per row or request depends on the scraper, its settings, the complexity of the task, and the data source. What happens if my scraping request fails? If your scraping request fails, the credits used are automatically refunded. Some scraping APIs also include automatic retries, so the system can try again and complete the data extraction even if the first attempt runs into issues. What if I need more than my current subscription offers? If you need higher limits or a custom solution, contact our support team via live chat or email at info@hasdata.com. We'll work with you to find a flexible option that fits your needs. Do you provide custom scraping solutions? Yes, we will be happy to address your data collection requests, if you provide a specific website and the exact data points you need. We can't take on vague requests without clear sources and targets. Free to start, pay per success 1,000 API calls included. No credit card required.
