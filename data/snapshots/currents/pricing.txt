 Pricing questions 
 Is there a free News API plan? 
 Yes. The free developer plan includes 250 requests per day for evaluation, prototypes, and small apps. 
 When should I upgrade? 
 Upgrade when you need higher monthly volume, a longer history window, larger result limits, or production support. 
 Can I redistribute article content? 
 Self-service plans provide technical API access. Redistribution, republication, OEM use, or long-term archival use may require separate terms.

 Plan quotas are enforced daily and reset at 00:00 UTC. When a day's cap is reached, requests return HTTP 429 with a Retry-After header until the next reset. Unused daily volume does not roll over, and there is no overage billing.
