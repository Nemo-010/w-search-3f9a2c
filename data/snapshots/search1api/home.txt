Explore all pricing options Frequently Asked Questions Get quick answers about our APIs and agent interfaces Contact us at sys@search1api.com How quickly will I receive search results? Search results are typically delivered within 1-2 seconds. For most queries, you'll get instant responses that you can use in your applications. Which search engines are supported? The Search endpoint covers general web, developer and community, media and reference, and Chinese-language sources, including Google, Bing, GitHub, Reddit, YouTube, and WeChat. Set search_service in the request to pick an engine; see the Search API documentation for the current supported list. Why does some website crawling fail? Website crawling is a complex process, and we cannot guarantee that all websites can be crawled at all times. However, we have adapted to crawl popular websites, including YouTube, Reddits, GitHub, Medium, and WeChat Official Accounts. If you are a corporate user, we can provide customized services to optimize crawling for your frequently used websites, while improving the success rate and presentation of crawled content. How can I use this product? Search1API gives AI agents one web access layer for search, crawl, extract, sitemap, and trending workflows. You can integrate it directly over HTTP or connect through MCP, CLI tooling, and reusable skills in agent environments such as Claude, Cursor, Windsurf, and custom apps. How can I contact you? You can contact us through the official email or join our Discord community. How can I see the usage of my account? You can see the usage of your account by logging in to your account and checking the usage statistics in the dashboard. Do you store the search results? No, we don't store any search results. Each request is processed in real-time and the results are immediately returned to you. How are credits charged for failed requests? Failed requests should not deduct any credits from your account. If you notice any incorrect credit deductions, please contact us at sys@search1api.com for assistance. How does DeepSearch mode billing work? In DeepSearch mode, credits are charged based on the search query (1 credit) plus the number of successfully crawled pages. For example, if you set crawl_results=4 but only 3 pages are successfully crawled, you will be charged 1+3=4 credits instead of 1+4=5 credits. What is your refund policy? Purchases are non-refundable. You can cancel a subscription at any time from your dashboard — cancelling stops the next billing cycle, but the current period is not refunded. If the service doesn't match what this site describes, email us at sys@search1api.com and we'll issue a refund once we've confirmed it. The 3-day plan is excluded from that exception and is never refunded, including when it doesn't match the site description or your expectations. Start with one API.
