 Steer it with four headers 
 Options ride as headers, never in the body, so the call stays the vendor's own request. 
 X-Treg-Route-Prefer Try a vendor first, like free TinyFish. 
 X-Treg-Route-Exclude Skip a vendor that gave you bad answers. 
 X-Treg-Route-Max-Cost Cap one call. Without it, a miss can walk up to $1 of vendors. 
 X-Treg-Route-Waterfall Set to 0 to stop at the first miss.
