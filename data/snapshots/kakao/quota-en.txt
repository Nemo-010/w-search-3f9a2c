 This document guides the quota information for the Kakao Developers. 
 Quotas are counted as either the number of requests or data throughput, depending on the usage of the App. Some APIs are restricted in terms of request or quota depending on permissions. The quota information can be found below. 
 App's current quotas: [My Applications] > [Statistics] > [Quotas] 
 Requesting a quota increase or permission: [Concepts document in the applicable API] > [Usage policy]

 From February 2, 2026, to December 31, 2027, 10 KRW per call is applied with an 80% discount.
