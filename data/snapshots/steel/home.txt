Steel is cloud browser infrastructure for AI agents. It gives agents isolated cloud browser sessions with built-in anti-bot stealth, CAPTCHA solving and session persistence. Is Steel open source? Yes. Steel Browser is the open-source server on GitHub that you can host yourself. Steel Cloud is the managed version, adding scale, stealth, proxies and CAPTCHA solving. Can I use Steel with Playwright, Puppeteer or Selenium? Yes. Steel works with Playwright, Puppeteer, Selenium and its own Node and Python SDKs. You connect over a standard CDP WebSocket URL, so any CDP-compatible tool works out of the box. How fast does Steel start a browser session? Under a second on average, with no cold-start penalty. Each session launches into its own isolated Chromium in the cloud, with its proxy, cookies and storage already set up. Can Steel bypass CAPTCHAs? Yes, on Steel Cloud. With CAPTCHA solving on, a session detects and solves CAPTCHAs as your agent browses, including Cloudflare Turnstile, PerimeterX press-and-hold and image CAPTCHAs, and the CAPTCHAs API reports each one's progress. Stealth and proxies keep many from appearing at all. Does Steel support human-in-the-loop workflows and reusing logins? Yes. A live session viewer lets people watch or take over running sessions. Persistent browser profiles keep cookies and session tokens, so agents pick up signed-in workflows without logging in again. How much does Steel cost, and is there a free tier? Launch includes $30 free credits, no monthly fee, and usage-based pricing after your one-time credits run out. Scale is $250 per month plus usage, and Enterprise pricing is custom.
