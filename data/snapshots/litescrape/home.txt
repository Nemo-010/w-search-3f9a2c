You send a query and a location, and Litescrape returns organic results, Knowledge Graph panels, and rich SERP modules from Google, Bing, or DuckDuckGo without running browsers or proxies yourself. How much does the Litescrape SERP API cost? A flat $0.15 per 1,000 requests for every endpoint. There are no plans, seats, or monthly fees. Prepay from $10, which buys 66,667 requests, and the credits last six months. Is there a free SERP API tier? Every new visitor key comes with 5 free calls and no card is required. Use them in the playground on this page or from your own code, then top up the same key when you need more. How does Litescrape compare with Serper, SerpApi, and Scrapingdog on price? Serper's entry pack works out to $1.00 per 1,000 searches and its credits expire after six months. SerpApi's Starter plan is $25 a month for 1,000 searches. All as typed JSON. Do I need a subscription or a credit card to start? No. Your first 5 calls are free with no card. After that you buy credit top-ups through Stripe starting at $10.
