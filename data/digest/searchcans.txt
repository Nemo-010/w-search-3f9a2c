## What is SearchCans?
SearchCans is AI data infrastructure providing real-time search results (SERP API) and LLM-ready web content extraction (Reader API) from one unified platform. Built for AI agents, RAG systems, and developers who need reliable, affordable access to web data.
SearchCans is powered by Vcans, where its scalable search and extraction workflows run.
**Core Value**: Prepaid credit packs from $18, down to $0.56 per 1,000 credits on Ultimate. Standard SERP uses 1 credit; standard Reader uses 2 credits ($1.12 per 1,000 requests on Ultimate), before proxy add-ons. Parallel Lanes limit in-flight concurrency, with no hourly request caps. 100 free credits for new users.
