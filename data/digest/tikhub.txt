# TikHub
> TikHub is a social media data infrastructure platform providing 1,000+ real-time API endpoints across 16 social platforms, 1B+ record datasets for AI training, 990+ MCP tools for AI agents, and a real-time Live Room WebSocket API — serving developers, AI agents, researchers, and enterprises.
TikHub focuses on social data infrastructure for the AI era: social reasoning and emotional-intelligence datasets for physical AI, plus real-time social APIs powering AI agents in quantitative trading, government & defense, and marketing. 30,000+ developers and data teams use TikHub; 300M+ requests are ingested daily. Public data only — GDPR & CCPA aligned.
Supported platforms (16): TikTok, Douyin, Xigua, Instagram, YouTube, Twitter/X, Rednote (XHS / Xiaohongshu), Threads, LinkedIn, Reddit, Bilibili, Weibo, Kuaishou, WeChat, Lemon8, Zhihu.
Languages: the whole site is published in English (tikhub.io/), Simplified Chinese (/zh), French (/fr) and Spanish (/es). Every page exists at the same path in all four trees — /pricing, /zh/pricing, /fr/pricing, /es/pricing — and the English URL is the x-default.
Pricing: pay-as-you-go from $0.001/request with automatic daily volume discounts (down to $0.0005 at 30,000+ requests/day); $0.05 free sign-up credit (~50 requests, no credit card, granted once per user); default 10 RPS rate limit, upgradeable to 100+ RPS; Enterprise from $3,000 one-time or $4,500 cumulative spend. Payment methods: PayPal (guest checkout), Alipay, USDT.
