# Scite API
- [Documentation](https://docs.scite.ai/introduction.md): Build tools to find, understand, and use scientific research with Scite.
- [Quickstart](https://docs.scite.ai/quickstart.md): Retrieve a paper, check its citation tally, and run your first authenticated search.
- [Get API key](https://docs.scite.ai/authentication.md): Authenticate with an API key or Enterprise credentials.
- [For agents](https://docs.scite.ai/agents.md): Give ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft 365 Copilot, and other AI agents access to Scite documentation and citation data.
- [Glossary](https://docs.scite.ai/concepts/glossary.md): Quick reference for Scite-specific terminology used across the API.
- [Citations & Tallies](https://docs.scite.ai/concepts/citation-model.md): How Scite models citations: targets, sources, Smart Citations, and tallies.
- [Search](https://docs.scite.ai/guides/search.md): Query Scite's publication metadata and citation data with Boolean terms and 25+ filters.
- [Smart Citations](https://docs.scite.ai/guides/smart-citations.md): Get tallies, browse the citation graph, and look up journal-level Smart Citation data.
- [Scite Zotero plugin](https://docs.scite.ai/guides/zotero-plugin.md): Install Scite's free Zotero plugin to see Smart Citation tallies and open Scite Reports from your Zotero library.
- [Papers & Authors](https://docs.scite.ai/guides/papers-and-authors.md): Look up publication metadata by DOI or PMID, and author-level metadata and citation statistics.
- [Overview](https://docs.scite.ai/guides/evidence/overview.md): Search patents, grants, clinical trials, and regulatory records alongside citation data.
- [Patents](https://docs.scite.ai/guides/evidence/patents.md): Search patent filings with family, forward-citation, assignee, and legal-event data.
- [Clinical Trials](https://docs.scite.ai/guides/evidence/clinical-trials.md): Search registered clinical trials with phase, status, sponsor, and intervention filters.
- [Grants](https://docs.scite.ai/guides/evidence/grants.md): Search research grant awards from NIH RePORTER, NSF, and other funding sources.
- [Drugs](https://docs.scite.ai/guides/evidence/drugs.md): Search FDA drug labels, Orange Book listings, and Drugs@FDA application records.
- [Device Clearances](https://docs.scite.ai/guides/evidence/devices.md): Search FDA 510(k) device clearance decisions and their summary PDFs.
- [Adverse Events](https://docs.scite.ai/guides/evidence/adverse-events.md): Search FDA MAUDE device and FAERS drug adverse-event reports.
