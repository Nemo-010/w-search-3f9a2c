- [Geo Targeting](https://scrape.do/documentation/proxies/geo-code.md): Target specific countries with Scrape.do geo-targeting to access location-restricted content and avoid regional IP blocking.
- [Regional Geo Targeting](https://scrape.do/documentation/proxies/regional-geo-code.md): Use Scrape.do regional geo-targeting to route requests through continents like Europe, Asia, or North America for improved success.
- [Postal Code (ZIP Code) Targeting](https://scrape.do/documentation/proxies/postal-code.md): Target specific postal codes and ZIP codes with Scrape.do for hyper-local geo-targeting. Combine with country-level geoCode for precise location-based scraping.
