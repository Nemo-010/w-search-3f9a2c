# Scrapingdog
> Scrapingdog is a web scraping API platform that handles proxies, CAPTCHAs, headless browsers, and JavaScript rendering so developers can extract structured data from any website at scale without getting blocked.
Scrapingdog provides 60+ scraping and data-extraction APIs covering Google Search, Amazon, social media, e-commerce, AI/LLM pipelines, and more. All APIs return clean, structured JSON. Plans start with a free trial and scale to millions of requests per month.

## Google APIs
- [Google SERP API](https://www.scrapingdog.com/google-serp-api/): Scrape Google search results — organic listings, ads, People Also Ask, knowledge graph, and more.
- [Google AI Mode API](https://www.scrapingdog.com/google-ai-mode-api/): Extract AI-generated summaries from Google's AI Mode search results.
- [Google AI Overview API](https://www.scrapingdog.com/documentation/google-ai-overview-api/): Scrape Google AI Overview snippets that appear at the top of search results.
- [Google Immersive Product API](https://www.scrapingdog.com/google-immersive-product-api/): Extract product data from Google's immersive product panels.
- [Google Maps Search API](https://www.scrapingdog.com/documentation/google-maps-search-api/): Scrape Google Maps business listings, ratings, hours, and contact details.
- [Google Maps Posts API](https://www.scrapingdog.com/documentation/google-maps-posts-api/): Extract Google Business Profile posts for any Maps listing.
- [Google Maps Reviews API](https://www.scrapingdog.com/documentation/google-maps-reviews-api/): Scrape customer reviews and ratings from Google Maps listings.
- [Google Maps Photos API](https://www.scrapingdog.com/documentation/google-maps-photos-api/): Retrieve photos associated with Google Maps business listings.
