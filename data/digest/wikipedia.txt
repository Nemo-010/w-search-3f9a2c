[docs] Wikipedia and other Wikimedia projects are free, collaborative repositories of knowledge, written and maintained by volunteers around the world. Wikimedia APIs give you open access to use this free knowledge in your projects and apps.
[docs] Service for high-volume commercial reuse of Wikimedia content, including free community access
[docs] Wikimedia content is organized by project and language. Each project hosts a different type of free content—encyclopedia articles on Wikipedia, dictionary entries on Wiktionary—and can be published under a different free license.
