[billing-md] * **1,000 credits per month.** The allotment resets at the start of each monthly cycle. Unused free credits do not roll over.
[billing-md] You buy credits in increments of 5 USD. When your credit balance reaches zero, we add one increment to your account and charge your card on file.
[billing-md] You can also buy credits yourself at any time. Use **Load more credits** in your [billing settings](https://www.firecrawl.dev/app/settings?tab=billing). Enter a multiple of 5 USD, and pay with your card on file. This works whether pay-as-you-go is on or off.
[billing-md] | Plan | Credits per 5 USD |
[billing-md] | **Hobby, Standard, Growth, Scale** | On | Firecrawl charges your card for one 5 USD increment and adds its credits. Your requests continue. This repeats each time the balance reaches zero, until your **monthly pay-as-you-go limit** is reached. Leave the limit blank for no cap. |
[billing-md] For example, processing a typical post or thread request costs **30 credits** (`1` base + `29` Grok X Query) and returns Grok-generated structured data, thread context, and summaries. If JSON format (LLM extraction) is also enabled, the total is **34 credits** per request.
[billing-md] The free plan does not include pay-as-you-go, so requests return an HTTP 402 once the monthly allotment runs out. See [Running Out of Credits](#running-out-of-credits). It also does not include credit rollover, a Data Processing Agreement, or Slack support. Support is community support.
[billing-md] Scrape, Search and Interact also work with no API key at all, through the keyless free tier, which is capped per IP address per day rather than by a credit allotment. See [Keyless (no API key)](/rate-limits#keyless-no-api-key).
[billing-md] Your limit is the most that pay-as-you-go can spend in one month. It rounds down to whole 5 USD increments.
[billing-md] For example, a limit of 25 USD allows five increments each month. A limit of 22 USD allows four increments, because 22 USD rounds down to 20 USD.

[billing-md] | **Scrape** | 1 credit / page | Convert a single URL into clean markdown, HTML, or structured data. Additional credits apply when using scrape options (see below). |
[billing-md] | **Crawl** | 1 credit / page | Scrape an entire website by following links from a starting URL. The same per-page scrape option costs apply to each page crawled. |
[billing-md] | **Map** | 1 credit / call | Discover all URLs on a website without scraping their content. |
[billing-md] | **Search** | 2 credits / 10 results | Search the web and optionally scrape the results. Rounded up per 10 results (e.g., 11 results = 4 credits). Additional per-page scrape costs apply to each result that is scraped. See [here](/features/search#zero-data-retention-zdr) for enterprise ZDR search pricing. |
[billing-md] | PDF parsing | +1 credit / PDF page | Extract content from PDF documents |
[billing-md] | JSON format (LLM extraction) | +4 credits / page | Use an LLM to extract structured JSON data from the page |
[billing-md] | Prompt injection check | +4 credits / page | Opt-in `checkPromptInjection` scrape option, available with any format (see [Prompt injection detection](/features/llm-extract#prompt-injection-detection)). If the scrape fails after the check has run, 5 credits are billed. See [When credits are charged](#when-credits-are-charged). |
[billing-md] | Zero Data Retention (ZDR) | +1 credit / page | Ensures no data is persisted beyond the request (see [Scrape ZDR](/features/scrape#zero-data-retention-zdr)) |
[billing-md] | `question` or `query` format | +4 credits / page | LLM-generated answer to a question about the page (see [Scrape](/features/scrape)) |
[billing-md] | `highlights` format | +4 credits / page | LLM-selected relevant passages from the page (see [Scrape](/features/scrape)) |
[billing-md] | `audio` format | +4 credits / page | Transcribe audio found on the page (see [Scrape](/features/scrape)) |
[billing-md] | `video` format | +4 credits / page | Transcribe video found on the page (see [Scrape](/features/scrape)) |
[billing-md] | PII redaction (`redactPII`) | +4 credits / page | Redact personal data from the returned markdown. Each additional PDF page adds another +4 on top of its +1 parsing cost (see [PII redaction](/features/pii-redaction)) |
[billing-md] | `lockdown` (cache hit) | +4 credits / page | Serve from cache only, never fetching the target. A cache miss returns no document and bills 1 credit (see [Lockdown](/features/lockdown) and the table below) |
[billing-md] | Enhanced proxy (`proxy: "enhanced"` or `auto` escalation) | +0 | Billed at the same 1 credit as a basic request. An escalated retry is not charged separately (see [Enhanced Mode](/features/enhanced-mode)) |
[billing-md] These modifiers stack. For example, scraping a page with both JSON format and Zero Data Retention costs **1 + 4 + 1 = 6 credits** per page, and JSON format with PII redaction costs **1 + 4 + 4 = 9 credits**. These same modifiers apply to the Crawl and Search endpoints since they use scrape internally for each page.
[billing-md] * **Firecrawl returned no document: 0 credits.** A scrape that fails outright, for example because the site never responded or every rendering attempt failed, is not charged.
[billing-md] | Threat protection scan | 2 credits per scanned URL, including a scrape that the scan itself blocked |
[billing-md] | Prompt injection check | 5 credits, once the `checkPromptInjection` guard has run |
[billing-md] | `lockdown` cache miss | 1 credit |
[billing-md] | Monitor check | A page that errors during a check is charged the base 1 credit per page |
